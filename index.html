The Gap

It has been harder than ever for young people and people of color to become homeowners in our country, in Virginia, and within Hampton Roads. This has EVERYTHING to do with race and age. It’s not all about money.

What do you mean by that?

Disclaimer: These statistics describe an overall pattern across different groups of people. Consequently, it does not account for individual circumstances or outliers. By becoming aware of these patterns, we can work towards making it fair for everyone despite historic and systematic barriers. Account for the fact that multiple of these factors can apply to any individual.
