Addressing this cause: "I want to help this cause"

If you want to help this cause, I encourage you to:

  • Advocate for policy changes at the local and state level. Specificially, encourage your state/local government to reform zoning and land use to allow more homes to be built.
  • Support organizations working to increase affordable housing options.
  • Engage in community discussions about housing equity.
  • Consider making a donation or volunteering your time to relevant causes.
